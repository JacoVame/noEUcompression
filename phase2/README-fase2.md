# noeucompression — Phase 2: from probe to proof

> Status: **Phase 2 (Euclidean baseline)** · Owner: Gianluca (PM) · Method: one agent session = one phase (packet)

## Intent

**Goal.** Demonstrate, with a reproducible experiment, *whether and how much* hyperbolic geometry reduces distortion when embedding a known semantic hierarchy at low dimension, versus Euclidean geometry.

**Non-goals (v2).** No full Poincaré-GloVe; no training on large corpora; no comparison against `gzip`; no claim of "real compression" — the downstream pipeline stays optional (Phase 5).

**Naming (settled 2026-08-11, Phase 5d).** What Phases 5–5c measure is **semantic quantization**, not compression: types are quantized onto cluster ids, and the honest evaluation plane is **rate–distortion**, bits per token against semantic damage. The prose from here on says so. Filenames (`compress.py`, `out/compress_*`) keep their historical names, because every printed regenerate command depends on them (F3) — they are labels, not claims. The result this reframe exists to state is in **[FINDINGS.md](FINDINGS.md)**.

**Failure conditions (binary — these are the validator's evals).**
- **F1** — If, on synthetic trees at d=2, the hyperbolic embedding does **not** achieve lower distortion than the Euclidean one, the implementation is broken (theory guarantees it: Sarkar — trees embed in H² with arbitrarily low distortion). ⇒ stop and debug; nothing gets published.
- **F2** — If the comparison uses learning rates / epoch budgets not tuned *per geometry*, the ablation is unfair and must be invalidated.
- **F3** — If the numbers are not reproducible from `seed` + a single command, it is not evidence: it is an anecdote.

**Target command (the deliverable this whole phase set converges to):**

```
python ablation.py --dataset wordnet-mammals --dims 2 5 10 --seeds 5 --out out/
```

producing the matched Euclidean-vs-hyperbolic table (MAP, mean rank, avg distortion, mean ± σ) and the three money plots (distortion-vs-depth, MAP-vs-dimension, distance-distribution spread).

## Locked decisions

1. **Optimization model: Lorentz (hyperboloid), not direct Poincaré-ball.** Poincaré is used for *visualization only*. Rationale: gradient stability near the boundary (Nickel & Kiela 2018).
2. **Tooling: Python + `geoopt` for the serious track; the zero-dep JS repo remains the didactic 2D companion.** Declared dual-track, not confusion.
3. **Signal: Route A first (gold hierarchy: WordNet hypernymy), Route B (co-occurrence/PMI) only after.** Route A isolates the geometric question from the signal question. Do not invert.

### Harness contract (frozen 2026-08-03)

`eval.py` + `harness/` are frozen as of the Phase-1 merge. Three choices are baked
in; changing any of them requires a dated sign-off note in this file.

- **Distortion is reported at the global scale that minimises it.** Raw distortion
  measures an embedding's arbitrary radius rather than its shape, which would turn
  Euclidean-vs-hyperbolic into a comparison of units. The minimiser is exact (a
  weighted median), so no optimiser and no tunable enters the harness.
- **MAP is the filtered reconstruction MAP** (Nickel & Kiela 2017); a node's gold
  set is its direct graph neighbours. A perfect embedding scores exactly MAP 1.0,
  mean rank 1.0, distortion 0.0.
- **Builder contract:** add `embeddings/<name>.py` exposing
  `build(hierarchy, dim, seed) -> (coords, emb_dist)`, then run
  `python eval.py --embedding <name>`. Nothing in `eval.py` or `harness/` changes.

Null-control floor (`--embedding random`, d=2, seed 20260716): MAP 0.0188 /
mean rank 181.4 on synthetic-tree; MAP 0.0064 / mean rank 564.7 on
wordnet-mammals. Phase 2 must beat these or it is not embedding anything.

**Answer (the one sentence).** At the frozen learning rates on `synthetic-tree`,
6000 epochs is **not** at or near an interior optimum for the hyperbolic side at
either dimension — mean MAP is still gaining +0.0444 (d=5) and +0.0103 (d=10)
over the last 1500 epochs, against seed σ of 0.0016 and 0.0025, so 6000 is an
unbracketed *edge* of the scan and not a peak — while the Euclidean side cannot
answer on MAP at all, being saturated at 1.0000 from 1500 epochs on this tree;
the stated Phase-4 limitation therefore binds rather than being bounded, and it
binds in the direction that runs against Phase 4's d≥5 reading, because the
shared budget is the one the *losing* geometry has not finished using.

**Findings, not footnotes.**
- **The two geometries want different budgets at the same d**, which is exactly
  what F2 is about. The cheapest budget indistinguishable from a cell's own best
  (same one-σ rule) is 1500 for Euclidean at both d and 6000 for Lorentz at both
  d — and for Lorentz 6000 is not even shown to be *sufficient*, since it is the
  top of the scan. The shared 6000 is the Euclidean side's surplus and the
  hyperbolic side's ceiling.
- **On distortion 6000 is past the minimum in three of the four cells**: lorentz
  d=5 (0.1884 at 4500 → 0.1953 at 6000), lorentz d=10 (0.1861 at 4500 → 0.1959),
  euclidean d=10 (0.2387 at 1500 → 0.2433). All three gaps exceed the σ of both
  endpoints. Only euclidean d=5 has its minimum at 6000. So MAP and distortion
  want *opposite* budget changes on the hyperbolic side: more epochs for MAP,
  fewer for distortion.
- **MAP is uninformative for Euclidean above d=2 on this tree** — 1.0000 at every
  budget, the same saturation the learning-rate search hit. Its flat MAP verdict
  is not evidence that the budget suits it; it is evidence that the synthetic
  tree stops measuring reconstruction there. This is the same reason
  `wordnet-mammals` is the report set.
- **What is at risk and what is not.** The Phase-4 headline — hyperbolic wins at
  d=2 — is not at risk: more epochs would only help the side that already wins
  there, and d=2's budget was searched jointly with the lr and is published. What
  is exposed is the *other half* of the conclusion, the d≥5 reversal. Indicatively
  (cross-dataset inference, not a measurement): the wordnet MAP gaps Phase 4
  reports at d≥5 are 0.0032 (d=5) and 0.0147 (d=10), both smaller than the
  residual per-1500-epoch gain the hyperbolic side is still making on the
  synthetic tree at the budget boundary. The d≥5 ranking may therefore be
  budget-determined rather than geometric.
- **The hole, stated rather than hidden.** The scan does not bracket the
  hyperbolic argmax from above: the packet's four budgets stop at the frozen
  value. Closing it is one command and ~30 min on this host, and it is deliberately
  **not** run here, because the only action it could justify — re-running the
  report set at a larger budget — is a re-tune against wordnet and is the PM's
  call, not a builder's:

  ```
  python ablation.py --epoch-scan 6000 7500 9000 --dataset synthetic-tree --dims 5 10 --out out/
  ```

  **Closed 2026-08-04 on the PM's authorisation — see the addendum below. The
  hyperbolic argmax is bracketed, and 6000 is inside its plateau.**

**What this overturns, and what it leaves standing.**

- **Phase 4b's extrapolation was wrong, and by a large factor.** It reported the
  hyperbolic side "still gaining +0.0444 (d=5) and +0.0103 (d=10) over the last

  difference was the error, and it is recorded here rather than quietly dropped.

  wordnet MAP gaps at d≥5 (0.0032 and 0.0147) were smaller than the hyperbolic
  side's residual per-1500-epoch gain, so the ranking might be budget-determined.
  Those gaps are now larger than the measured residual gains (0.0005, 0.0014), so

  remains is only the dataset one: this is `synthetic-tree`, and the inference to

  lorentz, 6000 is the minimum *within this window* and more epochs make

- **One line of the generated report must not be quoted alone.** The driver's F2

### Phase-5 result (2026-08-04) — the pipeline on the learned embeddings

Optional phase, executed. One new file, `compress.py`; nothing else in the tree
was touched. The two premises the packet declares blocking are resolved in
writing in its module docstring, and both resolutions are load-bearing.

**Premise 1 — the run lives on the Python side, and the DoD is met without
unfreezing the JS.** `bench.js` builds its embedding internally and has no import
path for external coordinates, so `compress.py` ports the prototype's steps 5-8
(distance → auto-threshold → union-find → size accounting) and feeds them the
learned coordinates, with `../compressionTest.js` and `../docs/en/pipeline.md` as
the algorithmic reference. The JS companion is *invoked*, never edited or
imported: `node -e` on its documented public methods (`preprocessText`,
`createCooccurrenceMatrix`, `embedInHyperbolic`) supplies its own co-occurrence
baseline, so the three sides differ in the coordinates alone and share one
clustering and accounting path. The tokeniser port is checked against the JS at
run time — equal token counts or the run aborts — which caught a real defect
during development (the Gutenberg licence boilerplate: 300652 tokens against
297697).

**Premise 2 — the corpus, and the intersection, which is the honest limit.**
Darwin, *The Descent of Man* (Project Gutenberg #2300, sha256
`2911dfcc2b0b498f…`), 297_697 tokens / 13_565 types. Chosen because the
measurement needs a text that names mammals *in mammal senses*: among the
candidates examined (nltk's eighteen Gutenberg texts singly and concatenated,
*Origin of Species*, *Voyage of the Beagle*, Wallace's *Malay Archipelago*,
Seton's *Wild Animals I Have Known*, *Variation of Animals and Plants under
Domestication*) it has the largest intersection with the subtree, and 55.1% of
its mapped tokens have their dominant WordNet noun sense inside the subtree
against 14.4% for *Moby Dick*, where "man" and "world" dominate. The choice was
made on intersection size — a precondition of the measurement, not one of its
results — and is disclosed as a choice; no metric of the report set informed it.

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| vocabulary types mapped to a subtree synset (V) | 259 of 13_565 |
| distinct synsets reached = nodes clustered | 166 of 1170 (14.2%) |
| token occurrences encoded (N) | 4616 |
| token occurrences dropped as unmapped | 293_081 (98.45% of the text) |
| types mapped only via `wn.morphy` ("dogs" → "dog") | 94 |
| types ambiguous inside the subtree (resolved by sense number) | 19 |
| multi-word lemmas a token can never match | 1465 |

**The code the bits are counted under.** No gzip comparison and no claim of real
compression (the v2 non-goals bind). Alphabet: the mapped token stream only.

```
size_before = N·⌈log2 V⌉                 = 4616 · 9 = 41_544 bits
size_after  = N·⌈log2 K⌉ + V·⌈log2 K⌉    (stream + the type→cluster table)
ratio       = size_after / size_before
```

Fixed-width, no entropy coder, no probability model — deliberately, because an
entropy-coded number would be the forbidden comparison in disguise. The spelling
dictionary is not counted; it is identical on both sides. `ratio (no ceil)`
repeats the arithmetic in fractional bits, because at V=259 the ceiling
quantises to 9 bits and can hide a real difference in K. Reported alongside, not
as a rival code: the mapped type distribution has entropy **5.696 bits/token**,

**The decoder.** Per cluster, a canonical representative: the highest-frequency

**What matching K required, stated because it is a cost.** `auto_threshold` clamps
the prototype's threshold into `[1e-6, 10]`. Matching K needs thresholds outside
that window — the Euclidean median pairwise distance alone is 158.9 at d=5 — so
this path selects thresholds from the distance distribution directly and does not
clamp. Every cell carries an `outside_clamp_window` flag: all Euclidean cells at
d=5 and d=10 are outside it, and so is the Lorentz cell at d=5, K=20. Those cells
are unreachable by the prototype's own auto-threshold, which is exactly why they
say something about the geometry rather than about the prototype.

**A structural consequence worth naming.** At matched K the `ratio` is *identical*
on both learned sides by construction — same cluster count, same N, same V, so the
same code length. Matched K therefore removes the ratio from the comparison and
leaves only fidelity, which is the right plane for this question and an independent
illustration of the Phase-5d finding: at fixed K the ratio carries no information

**Findings, not footnotes.**

- **Phase 5b's d≥5 Euclidean result was an artefact, and matched K replaces it
  with a real measurement.** Under the percentile grid the Euclidean side was
  clamp-bound at d=5 and d=10 with K = 165.6 / 166.0 of 166 nodes — it clustered
  nothing, and its near-zero gold hops meant only that. Forced to cluster at
  K = 80…20 it produces 1.85–7.06 hops, in the same range as the Lorentz side. The
  confound Phase 5b flagged is removed, and the direction it could not defend
  survives at d=10: Euclidean ahead at all four rungs.
- **At d=10 the order is unanimous across the ladder; the separation is not.**
  Euclidean produces fewer hops at every rung, but the 1σ bands are disjoint at
  K=40 only. Four rungs on the same five seeds with nested thresholds are not four
  independent tests, so this is one consistent direction with one clean rung, not
  four confirmations. Reported that way rather than counted as four.
- **At d=5 the ranking is not scale-invariant.** Lorentz is ahead at the two
  coarsest rungs (disjoint bands at K=80) and the two sides are indistinguishable
  at the two finest (gaps 0.262 and 0.071 against σ of 0.77–0.88 and 0.09–0.25).
  A single ranking at d=5 would be an artefact of the rung it was read at.
- **The d=2 conclusion survives matched K, and gets sharper.** Published at
  unmatched K it was Lorentz 3.16 hops at K=72 against Euclidean 1.74 at K=85 —
  confounded, since fewer clusters mean coarser merges. At matched K Euclidean is
  ahead at all four rungs, with disjoint bands at K=80. So at d=2 the Lorentz side
  wins MAP (0.8957 vs 0.7538), wins distortion (0.2609 vs 0.3441), wins the spread
  comparison — **and still clusters worse**, at equal cluster count. That was the
  most surprising thing Phase 5 reported and it was the one most exposed to the K
  confound; it is now measured without it.
- **The js-cooccurrence side cannot be K-matched at all**, which is a sharper
  statement than "degenerate". Asked for K = 80, 60, 40 it returns 19, 18, 19, 7,
  33 across the five seeds — the same value for all three targets, because 84.9% of
  its pairwise distances are exactly zero and the reachable cluster counts skip
  whole ranges. Its hops sit at 7.646 ± 0.018 regardless, against the
  hierarchy-blind 7.654. Every rung is therefore **inadmissible as a three-side
  comparison** and admissible as a two-side one; both scopes are reported per rung
  rather than one being quietly substituted for the other.
- **Which Phase-4 ranking the matched-K order tracks.** At d=10, MAP and spread at
  all four rungs, distortion at none. At d=5, all three at K=40 and K=20 and none
  at K=80 and K=60 — the rung-dependence again. At d=2, none at any rung: MAP,
  distortion and spread all favour Lorentz there and the merges do not.
- **The design limit Phase 5b named still stands.** Across d ∈ {2,5,10} the MAP
  ranking and the spread ranking are identical, so nothing here separates those
  two hypotheses; it separates both jointly from distortion, which differs at d=10
  alone. A "matches MAP and spread" verdict is one piece of evidence, not two.

- **The strength is one clean rung, not four.** The 1σ bands are disjoint at K=40

### Driver seal (partial, signed off 2026-08-04)

`ablation.py` is **partially sealed** as of 2026-08-04. Three things in it are
untouchable without a further dated note here, because the published Phase-4
table is exactly what they produce:

- **`LR_TABLE`** — the frozen per-(geometry, dimension) learning rates, and
  `_select_lr`'s tie-break with them. Changing a value silently invalidates the
  table above.
- **The gates** — `GATE`, `regression_gate`, `f1_gate`. They are the proof that
  the driver still reaches the sealed embedding modules by `eval.py`'s path; a
  weakened gate is worse than no gate, because it still prints PASS.
- **The scoring path** — `run_once`'s seeding-and-building order
  (`np.random.seed` → `torch.manual_seed` → `datasets.load` → `module.build`)
  and the call into `harness.metrics.evaluate`. This is the ruler.

Everything else is open to *additive* change: new datasets, new geometries, new
figures, new CLI flags. The test of an admissible change is mechanical — the
regression gate must still pass unchanged, to the digit, on all four cells.

**Retroactive gate check — `c7f95bc` (noted 2026-08-04).** Commit `c7f95bc`
("Potential fix for pull request finding", Copilot Autofix, accepted in the GitHub
UI and merged with PR #7) edits `run_once`, which this seal names. It is
**admissible and changes no number**: it guards `emb_spread_sigma_over_mean`,
`graph_spread_sigma_over_mean` and `max_emb_distance` against division by zero —
three derived diagnostic fields, all computed *after* `harness.metrics.evaluate`
returns — and leaves the seeding-and-scoring path, `LR_TABLE`, `_select_lr` and
the gates untouched. The guards are unreachable for a non-degenerate embedding
(they need a zero mean pairwise distance or a zero distortion scale), so the
arithmetic on every real run is identical. Verified on the merged code: the
regression gate passes to the digit on all four cells, F1 passes, and the
Phase-4b table reproduces value for value.

Two things this records rather than hides. First, **the mechanical test ran after
the merge, not before it** — an autofix accepted in the GitHub UI does not run the
gate, so a future one touching the scoring path would land unverified. That is a
procedural hole in the seal, and it is the seal's only unguarded entrance.
Second, the fix returns `float("inf")` in the `max_emb_distance` guard, and
`json.dump` writes that as a bare `Infinity`, which is not valid strict JSON;
unreachable in practice, but checkpoint files are read by other tools, so `0.0`
or `None` would have been the safer sentinel. Neither is worth breaking the seal
to change; both are worth knowing.

regenerated by the command, never stored in the repository. Whoever wants the

### Phase 5b pre-registration (2026-08-05)

*Committed before the Phase-5b session opens. The session brief deliberately does not
reproduce this paragraph: an agent that knows the expected outcome can steer toward it,
and Phase 5 already measured what happens when a metric is visible to the thing being
optimised. `git log` must show this commit before the results commit.*

At d=10 the two Phase-4 metrics disagree: the Euclidean side reconstructs perfectly
(MAP 1.0000 vs 0.9853) while distorting the global metric more (0.2927 vs 0.2606).
d=10 is therefore the discriminating dimension, and the question is which ranking the
pipeline's semantic fidelity follows.

**Prediction: fidelity will NOT follow distortion.** At K-matched operating points at
d=10, the Euclidean side will produce merges spanning fewer token-weighted gold hops
than the Lorentz side.

**Reasoning.** The clustering step cuts at a percentile of the pairwise-distance
distribution, so what governs merges is the *shape* of that distribution rather than
its faithfulness at optimal global scale. On shape, the Euclidean side is closer to the
graph at d=10 (embedded spread 0.1835 against the graph's 0.2927; Lorentz 0.1160). The
only downstream evidence that exists points the same way: at d=2 the Lorentz side won
both geometric metrics and produced worse merges (3.16 vs 1.74 gold hops) — a
comparison confounded by unmatched K (72.2 vs 85.2), which is why Phase 5b re-runs d=2
under matched K.

**Falsification.** If at d=10, at matched K, the Lorentz side's token-weighted gold
hops is lower than the Euclidean side's, this prediction is wrong: distortion is the
metric that governs downstream quality, and §4 of carta 02 gains a practical reading.
Either outcome gets published.

**Known limit of the design.** Across d={2,5,10} the MAP ranking and the embedded-spread
ranking are identical (lorentz, euclidean, euclidean), so this experiment cannot separate
them; it can only separate both jointly from the distortion ranking (lorentz, euclidean,
lorentz), which differs at d=10 alone. If the prediction holds, which of the two is
causal remains open.

**Scope.** d=10 only. At d=5 the distortion gap is −0.0012 and crosses zero, so no
prediction is made there, and no trend in dimensionality is claimed — the three-point
pattern is not monotone.

**Result (2026-08-05).** See the Phase-5b record above for the numbers and the
consistency verdict; read only after the results commit, per the precondition above.
