# noeucompression — Phase 2: from probe to proof

> Status: **Phase 2 (Euclidean baseline)** · Owner: Gianluca (PM) · Method: one agent session = one phase (packet)

## Intent

**Goal.** Demonstrate, with a reproducible experiment, *whether and how much* hyperbolic geometry reduces distortion when embedding a known semantic hierarchy at low dimension, versus Euclidean geometry.

**Non-goals (v2).** No full Poincaré-GloVe; no training on large corpora; no comparison against `gzip`; no claim of "real compression" — compression stays downstream and optional (Phase 5).

**Failure conditions (binary — these are the validator's evals).**
- **F1** — If, on synthetic trees at d=2, the hyperbolic embedding does **not** achieve lower distortion than the Euclidean one, the implementation is broken (theory guarantees it: Sarkar — trees embed in H² with arbitrarily low distortion). ⇒ stop and debug; nothing gets published.
- **F2** — If the comparison uses learning rates / epoch budgets not tuned *per geometry*, the ablation is unfair and must be invalidated.
- **F3** — If the numbers are not reproducible from `seed` + a single command, it is not evidence: it is an anecdote.

**Target command (the deliverable this whole phase set converges to):**

```
python ablation.py --dataset wordnet-mammals --dims 2 5 10 --seeds 5 --out out/
```

producing the matched Euclidean-vs-hyperbolic table (MAP, mean rank, avg distortion, mean ± σ) and the three money plots (distortion-vs-depth, MAP-vs-dimension, distance-distribution spread).

## Locked decisions

1. **Optimization model: Lorentz (hyperboloid), not direct Poincaré-ball.** Poincaré is used for *visualization only*. Rationale: gradient stability near the boundary (Nickel & Kiela 2018).
2. **Tooling: Python + `geoopt` for the serious track; the zero-dep JS repo remains the didactic 2D companion.** Declared dual-track, not confusion.
3. **Signal: Route A first (gold hierarchy: WordNet hypernymy), Route B (co-occurrence/PMI) only after.** Route A isolates the geometric question from the signal question. Do not invert.

### Harness contract (frozen 2026-08-03)

`eval.py` + `harness/` are frozen as of the Phase-1 merge. Three choices are baked
in; changing any of them requires a dated sign-off note in this file.

- **Distortion is reported at the global scale that minimises it.** Raw distortion
  measures an embedding's arbitrary radius rather than its shape, which would turn
  Euclidean-vs-hyperbolic into a comparison of units. The minimiser is exact (a
  weighted median), so no optimiser and no tunable enters the harness.
- **MAP is the filtered reconstruction MAP** (Nickel & Kiela 2017); a node's gold
  set is its direct graph neighbours. A perfect embedding scores exactly MAP 1.0,
  mean rank 1.0, distortion 0.0.
- **Builder contract:** add `embeddings/<name>.py` exposing
  `build(hierarchy, dim, seed) -> (coords, emb_dist)`, then run
  `python eval.py --embedding <name>`. Nothing in `eval.py` or `harness/` changes.

Null-control floor (`--embedding random`, d=2, seed 20260716): MAP 0.0188 /
mean rank 181.4 on synthetic-tree; MAP 0.0064 / mean rank 564.7 on
wordnet-mammals. Phase 2 must beat these or it is not embedding anything.

### Phase-4 result (2026-08-04) — the report set

`wordnet-mammals`, seeds 20260716..20260720 (5), mean ± σ, per-geometry LR frozen
before the run:

| d | geometry | lr | MAP | mean rank | avg distortion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | euclidean | 0.5 | 0.7538 ± 0.0028 | 5.5719 ± 0.6383 | 0.3441 ± 0.0155 |
| 2 | lorentz | 0.005 | 0.8957 ± 0.0032 | 13.8542 ± 1.4477 | 0.2609 ± 0.0043 |
| 5 | euclidean | 0.5 | 0.9812 ± 0.0019 | 1.0606 ± 0.0124 | 0.2593 ± 0.0074 |
| 5 | lorentz | 0.003 | 0.9780 ± 0.0009 | 4.1747 ± 0.2240 | 0.2605 ± 0.0009 |
| 10 | euclidean | 0.5 | 1.0000 ± 0.0000 | 1.0000 ± 0.0000 | 0.2927 ± 0.0015 |
| 10 | lorentz | 0.003 | 0.9853 ± 0.0011 | 2.4549 ± 0.1805 | 0.2606 ± 0.0009 |

**Conclusion.** Hyperbolic geometry wins unambiguously only in the
dimension-starved regime — at d=2 it lifts MAP 0.7538 → 0.8957 and cuts average
distortion 0.3441 → 0.2609 — because from d=5 on the Euclidean baseline takes
reconstruction outright (at d=10, MAP 1.0000 / mean rank 1.0000 against 0.9853 /
2.4549), while its distortion edge neither vanishes nor closes monotonically
(+0.0831 at d=2, −0.0012 at d=5, +0.0320 at d=10).

**F2 (2026-08-04).** Answered by option (b): the learning rate is tuned per
(geometry, dimension) on `synthetic-tree` only, best mean MAP over seeds
20260716/12345/777, frozen in `LR_TABLE` inside `ablation.py` before any wordnet
run. The Lorentz optimum moves with dimension (0.005 → 0.003), so reusing the
d=2 constant would have handicapped the hyperbolic side at the dimensions under
test. **Stated limitation:** the epoch budget is *not* tuned per dimension — it
stays at 6000 for both geometries at every d (the d=2 optimum for both), dropped
for runtime. That is the one axis on which this ablation is weaker than F2's
ideal reading.

**Findings, not footnotes.**
- Mean rank never inverts: hyperbolic is worse at every d (13.85 vs 5.57 at d=2,
  4.17 vs 1.06 at d=5, 2.45 vs 1.00 at d=10). At d=2 that coexists with a better
  MAP; from d=5 Euclidean leads both. The mechanism is in the spread figure:
  hyperbolic is *under*-spread (σ/mean 0.135 against the graph's 0.293), packing
  pairs into a narrow shell where non-neighbours sit at nearly the gold
  neighbour's distance; Euclidean at d=2 fails the opposite way (0.513).
- `MAX_SPATIAL_NORM 1e4` stops binding hard above d=2: 721.4/1170 nodes (61.7%)
  at d=2, 83.8 (7.2%) at d=5, 75.4 (6.4%) at d=10, with max embedding distance
  ~19.8 throughout.
- The Euclidean baseline *saturates* on `synthetic-tree` at d≥5 (MAP and mean
  rank both exactly 1.0 at every grid lr), which is why `wordnet-mammals` and not
  the synthetic tree is the report set.
- F1 does not fire: re-checked on `synthetic-tree` at d=2 (hyperbolic distortion
  0.2060 vs Euclidean 0.3773) before the sweep, which is gated on it.

**Regenerate** (artifacts land in `out/`, which is git-ignored, so the table and
the three figures are rebuilt rather than stored):

```
python ablation.py --dataset wordnet-mammals --dims 2 5 10 --seeds 5 --out out/
```

Aggregates spanning several seeds are tagged `seedset<base>x<k>`, which denotes
exactly `base, base+1, ..., base+k-1`; the seed list is also written verbatim
into the aggregate JSON. Cost on the 22-core build host: ~430 s per Euclidean
wordnet run and ~500-1100 s per Lorentz one, so a cold report set is ~3.5 h
sequential. Every run is checkpointed to
`out/ablation_run_<geometry>_<dataset>_d<dim>_seed<seed>.json` and reused when
its recorded lr/epochs match, so an interrupted sweep resumes.

### Handoff into Phases 5-6

What the next packet inherits, and what it must not re-decide:

- **The numbers above are the report set and it was run once.** Re-running the
  command reproduces them from the checkpoints; deleting `out/` reproduces them
  from scratch. Nothing in Phases 5-6 may re-tune against wordnet gold labels.
- **Phase 6 (article §4) has its chart already**: `out/ablation_map_vs_dimension_*`
  is the MAP-vs-dimension figure, `out/ablation_distortion_vs_depth_*` the depth
  curve, `out/ablation_distance_spread_*` the crowding comparison — all mean ± σ
  over the 5-seed set, none cherry-picked. The honest headline for the article is
  the d=2 result plus the d≥5 reversal, not the d=2 result alone.
- **Phase 5 (optional, compression) inherits a caveat**: the embedding that wins
  on distortion is not the one that wins on reconstruction above d=2, so "which
  embedding to compress with" is a real choice and must be stated, not assumed.
  The Lorentz coordinates are cap-bound for 6-7% of nodes at d≥5 (see above),
  which is a property of the numerical guard, not of the data.
- **Freeze status of `ablation.py` is undecided.** The harness freeze
  (`eval.py` + `harness/`) and the Phase-2/3 module seals stand as recorded. If
  the driver is to be sealed too, the PM should record it here with a date, the
  way the harness contract is recorded above.
